      What you are looking at

      The National Air Photo Library publishes georeferenced mosaics of Halifax for 1947 and 1977. These are historical aerial photographs, not reconstructed or generated scenes. Coverage is limited to the published footprint; blank areas can be outside that year’s imagery.

      Compare carefully

      The source says the mosaics are not necessarily precisely aligned with one another. Differences in capture date, resolution and alignment can make small features appear to move. Use the comparison to explore, not to measure property boundaries or establish exact construction dates.

      Official dataset, licence & original downloads ↗ · NRCan viewer ↗

      Find photographs from other years

      Halifax Municipal Archives indexes individual aerial photographs from 1955–2001. Open the index for the location above and select a point to see its archival record. The municipal archives exhibit explains the collection. For views of the harbour, ferry terminals and changing streets, browse the Flying to the Past photo album.

      Privacy and how the imagery loads

      The satellite image is included with this site. NRCan receives historical map-image requests for the area you view. OpenStreetMap receives background tile requests. Only photo references you choose to save are stored in this browser. Your shared URL contains the chosen map location. This app streams map-sized image tiles; it does not download the full original mosaics.

      The satellite view is a true-colour Sentinel-2C observation from 29 September 2025. Its roughly 10 m source pixels show the harbour and neighbourhoods. Zooming in enlarges those pixels. The street map is updated separately.
